How to use

  1. Prepare. Make sure the surface is clean and the paint is fully dry.
  2. First coat. Brush or sponge on a thin coat. It goes on milky and dries clear.
  3. More coats. Let each coat dry 2 to 4 hours, then apply the next. Use 2 to 3 thin coats in total.
  4. Cure. The finish cures in about 48 hours in dry conditions. Allow longer in humid weather or on damp surfaces, and keep it away from rain, sprinklers and washing until cured.

Thin coats work better than one thick coat. The finish can be painted over after full cure.

Where it works, and where it does not

Made for

  • Acrylic-painted rocks and stones
  • Terracotta and ceramic planters
  • Painted garden signs and outdoor crafts
  • Painted wood, fences and stepping stones
  • Concrete, brick, stone, plaster and walls
  • The outside and rim of concrete bird baths and fountains
  • Patching roof leaks: cracks, seams and around flashing

Not made for

  • Surfaces kept underwater: the inside of bird bath bowls, fountain basins, ponds or pools
  • Flat roof areas where water pools and stands for long periods
  • Blocking UV light: it is a clear protective coat that holds up to sun, not a UV-filtering coating

Safety

Questions

Does it yellow?

No. It dries clear with a matte finish and does not yellow.

Is it glossy or matte?

Matte.

How many coats do I need, and how long between them?

2 to 3 thin coats, 2 to 4 hours apart. It cures in about 48 hours in dry conditions; allow longer in humid weather or on damp surfaces.

How much does one jar cover?

Up to 14 square feet per 35 oz (1 kg) jar.

Can I use it inside a bird bath, fountain or pond?

No. It handles rain and splashes but is not made for surfaces kept underwater. It can be used on the outside and rim of concrete bird baths and fountains.

Can I use it to fix a roof leak?

Yes. Brush it over the cracks, seams or flashing where the roof leaks, on a clean, dry surface in dry weather, using 2 to 3 coats. Keep rain off until it cures, about 48 hours in dry conditions. It is not made for flat roof areas where water pools and stands for long periods.

Is it a spray?

No. It is a brush-on paste. A brush and gloves are included.

Is Tough Bison related to the Bison adhesives brand?

No. Tough Bison is a separate brand and is not affiliated with Bison adhesives and sealants from the Netherlands.
